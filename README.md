## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Computer Engineer and Full Stack Developer** passionate about building high-performance, scalable and user-friendly web applications.

I studied **Computer Engineering at Mehmet Akif Ersoy University** and have gained professional experience working on e-commerce platforms, corporate websites, influencer marketing systems, mobile applications, marketplace integrations and custom business solutions.

My core expertise is centered around **JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js and Node.js**, while I also work with relational and NoSQL databases, API integrations, Docker-based environments and modern frontend technologies.

In addition to software development, I have hands-on experience with e-commerce and digital advertising ecosystems including **Google Ads, Meta Ads, Trendyol Marketplace, İkas and Shopify**.

I continuously follow emerging technologies and integrate modern tools, development practices and automation solutions into the products I build.

Currently, I work at **StylingPark**, where I contribute to e-commerce development, digital infrastructure and technology-driven business solutions.

My previous professional experience includes projects developed at **Desird Design Arge**, **Acc Studio** and **Creamake E-Commerce Agency**.
