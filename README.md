## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Computer Engineer and Full Stack Developer** focused on building high-performance, scalable and user-friendly digital products.

I studied **Computer Engineering at Mehmet Akif Ersoy University** and have worked on e-commerce platforms, corporate websites, influencer marketing systems, mobile applications, marketplace integrations and custom business solutions.

My core expertise includes **JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js and Node.js**, alongside databases, API integrations, Docker environments and modern frontend technologies.

I also work with e-commerce and digital advertising ecosystems including **Google Ads, Meta Ads, Trendyol Marketplace, İkas and Shopify**.

Currently, I work at **StylingPark**. My previous experience includes projects developed at **Creamake E-Commerce Agency, Acc Studio and Desird Design Arge**.
